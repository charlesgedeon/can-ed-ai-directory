What the directory adds up to

Read one university's AI guidance and you learn about that university. Read forty and you start to see the shape of a national conversation, including the parts nobody is having. These are patterns worth arguing with, not findings to cite.

I don't have time to verify every claim below, and I'd rather say so than imply a rigour I haven't put in. The aim is to start conversations between the people doing this work, not to publish a literature review.
