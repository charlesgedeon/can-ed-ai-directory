What this is

A directory of the generative-AI policies, guidelines, and teaching resources published by Canadian post-secondary institutions, built so that teaching and learning units, educational developers, and instructors can find what peer institutions have said, compare approaches, and stop reinventing documents that already exist three provinces over.

Every institution in the country is listed, including the ones with nothing published. Those gaps are part of the picture.

How it's put together

The whole directory is one Google Sheet. Each row is a single published document, tagged with who issued it, who it addresses, and what kind of document it is. The site reads that sheet directly, so a correction goes live on the next page load rather than waiting on a code change.

Categories like issued by and audience are my reading of each document, not the institution's own label. Reasonable people will disagree with some of them, and I'd like to hear about it when they do.

Help keep it accurate

Missing resource, dead link, misread tag, new document published since I last looked — all of it is useful. Send it to me and I'll fold it in.
